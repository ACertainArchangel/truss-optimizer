This directory is the full deliverable of all the hard work with instructions and files for cutting! ud5 files are specific to certain printers, and it will work with the one at the InNEVation centre. Cut out the right number of parts, laminate together to the thickness specified in the list, and assemble according to the drawings, files, and directions.

This folder will have videos and photos of the final product if it has been built already. 

This should be all the documentation needed to remake the bridge once it is destroyed.

And finally, some specs.

* Material: 1/16 inch balsa from michaels (ours was specifically Guillows #5103)
* Epoxy: Sakrete high-strength anchoring epoxy 21500 PSI
* Gusset plates: 1/16 of an inch (one lamination layer) at each joint enough to cover and add support (see photos and videos)

Using the 1/8 balsa assumptions from the makerspace card just to make sure the cut is clean:

* Max power: 50%
* Min Power: 30%
* Passes: 1
* Kerf: 0.34 mm because of a 0.68 mm mat loss
* Speed: 25 mm/s
