\textbf{Hard won wisdom from bridge building:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Use wax paper under the bridge when assembling to prevent sticking to the work surface. It will save a major headache.
    \item Do not forget to use the kerf setting on the lazar! We calculated it in the ud5 files.
    \item Clamp well when curing epoxy, and cure at a temperature above room temperature for at least 24 hours. Cold temperatures will prevent proper curing and weaken the joints significantly.
    \item Go with the grain. Balsa is garbage if you put the grain the wrong way.
\end{itemize}
